## Naming convention (durable)

Engineers refactor general-purpose names to namespace-prefixed names as soon as
the first sibling concept appears. In Beta this happened at `RankingCalculator`
→ `TournamentRankingCalculator`. The guideline is now in `MEMORY.md` under
EPIC_04.

## Architecture constitution (durable)

- Capability pattern over exception throwers for placeholder surfaces.
- BracketValidator sits between BracketGenerator and TournamentService.
- Service orchestrates; validator validates; generator generates.
- No business rule in service. Match Engine remains single source of truth.
