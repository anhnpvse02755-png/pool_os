## Resolved Baseline Contract

`baseline_commit` is the full HEAD immediately before a Handoff checkpoint or
claim commit is authored. It cannot be the SHA of the commit containing the
Handoff because that would be a circular self-reference. A receiver verifies
that the baseline is an ancestor of checked-out HEAD, local HEAD equals the
remote-tracking HEAD, the Handoff is tracked at HEAD, and every post-baseline
commit and path matches the authorized checkpoint or claim scope. A claim
records the verified pre-claim HEAD as its new baseline. Post-checkpoint HEAD is
never required to equal `baseline_commit`.
