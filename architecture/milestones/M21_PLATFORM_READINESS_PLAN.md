# M21 Platform Readiness Plan

**Status:** Accepted Planning Baseline; Closed
**Date:** 2026-07-23

## Readiness Contract

M21 proves readiness of the frozen platform and prepares an explicit post-M22
Product boundary. It authorizes no Product feature, implementation or deployment.

## Dependency-Ordered Milestones

### M21.1 Readiness Identity & Scope
Freeze candidate, claims, boundaries, owners, evidence and exclusions.

### M21.2 Platform Completion Criteria
Define conjunctive constitutional, contract, enforcement and operational gates.

### M21.3 Contract & Freeze Readiness
Verify public compatibility and direct M20/transitive M3-M20 continuity.

### M21.4 Operational, Security & Recovery Readiness
Define attributable trust, failure, continuity and capacity readiness.

### M21.5 Evidence & Independent Audit Readiness
Require source-owned provenance, deterministic replay and independent audit.

### M21.6 Product Transition Boundary Preparation
Inventory allowed future public inputs/commands and prohibited internal bypasses.

### M21.7 Residual Gap & Exception Closure
Close, explicitly retain, or block every owned gap and expiring exception.

### M21.8 Final M22 Entry Gate
Record binary PO eligibility for separately authorized M22 planning only.

## Invariants

M20 Freeze is the direct root; M3-M20 remain protected. Domain truth and public
ports retain owners. Product stays locked until M22 Foundation Freeze is
accepted, closed, committed and pushed. Every M21.x requires separate authority.
