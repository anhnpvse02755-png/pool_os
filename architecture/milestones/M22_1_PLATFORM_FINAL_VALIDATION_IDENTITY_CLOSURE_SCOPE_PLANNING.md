## Immutable Final Validation Candidate Identity

A candidate binds:

1. candidate ID, schema version and declared final-validation target;
2. M21 manifest/proof identities and artifact-set digest;
3. accepted M22.0 plan, roadmap and proposed governance ADR identities;
4. included domains, public boundaries, contracts and capabilities;
5. constitutional, compatibility and completion determination identities;
6. freeze-chain, evidence, provenance, replay and audit package identities;
7. trust, privacy, operations, recovery and capacity identities;
8. residual gaps, findings, risks, exceptions and validity;
9. Product-transition prerequisites, owners and post-closure authorities;
10. rollback boundary, predecessor/successor lineage and digest.

Any bound change creates a successor. Names, paths, timestamps, implementation
status, green reports or provider output cannot replace semantic identity or
inherit validation/acceptance.

## Immutable Platform Closure Identity

Closure identity binds exact candidate/scope, constitutional/freeze/public-
contract determinations, conjunctive criteria, evidence/audit, resolved residuals,
Product prerequisites, ownership handoff, limitations, validity, PO decision,
rollback/supersession and digest. It is distinct from candidate identity and can
exist only after all source determinations are independently verified.

Closure identity records a governance result; it cannot close its own gaps,
repair evidence, create the M22 Freeze, authorize Product or execute transition.

## Exact Closure Scope

Scope is a first-class immutable object declaring included and excluded final
claims, domains, public boundaries, contract/version ranges, capabilities,
Knowledge/runtime identities, freeze roots, evidence/audit requirements,
security/privacy/operations/recovery/capacity classes, Product prerequisites,
residuals, owners, validity and rollback boundary.

Partial scope is valid only with explicit exclusions, unsupported/deferred
claims, rationale, accountable owner, evidence and impact. Omission is unknown,
not complete. Expansion, contraction, reclassification or owner change creates
a successor and repeats all affected validation gates.

## Inclusion And Exclusion Rules

- Include only public contracts and accepted source-owned determinations.
- Include positive and negative evidence for every declared closure claim.
- Include exact M21 root and transitive M3-M21 freeze identities.
- Exclude persistence, compiler, provider and domain runtime internals.
- Exclude Product features, UI, priority, Product data and runtime behavior.
- Exclude raw secrets and unnecessary player/Evidence content.
- Declare unknown, unsupported, degraded, exception-bound and not-applicable.
- Never infer closure across domains, versions, capabilities or omitted scope.
- Never treat generated reports or passing tests as normative authority.

## Validation Lineage And Successors

Lineage is append-only and acyclic:
`M21 Freeze -> M22.0 baseline -> M22.1 candidate -> successor`.
Each successor binds predecessor digest, exact change set, affected claims,
owner reauthorization, evidence, revalidation scope and new digest. Rejected,
failed and superseded candidates remain auditable; aliases cannot replace links.

Successors never inherit acceptance when root, scope, claim, contract, source,
owner, evidence, exception, validity or Product prerequisite changes.

## Compatibility Inheritance From M21 Freeze

Every candidate binds normalized M21 manifest/proof identities, artifact-set
digest and transitive M3-M21 protection status. Inheritance is by exact immutable
reference only; M22 cannot copy, regenerate, reinterpret or widen frozen claims.

M21 readiness determinations are historical inputs, not automatic final closure.
Missing, mixed, stale, broken or ambiguous anchors block the candidate. Changed
public semantics/version requires separate owner determination and successor.

## Immutable Evidence Bindings

Evidence references bind candidate/scope/claim, source semantic ID/digest,
contract/rule versions, canonical positive/negative result digests, owner and
custodian, method, validity, limitations, reviewer, correction/supersession
lineage and digest. Evidence remains at source; indexes/reports cannot self-review
or replace truth. Any binding change invalidates dependent verification.

## Rollback, Repair, Supersession And Fail-Closed Governance

Rollback selects a verified compatible predecessor candidate without editing
history. Repair occurs only at the accountable source under separate authority.
Supersession links candidate, scope, evidence, audit and decision digests.

Reject wrong/mixed/stale/duplicate identity, unknown scope/owner, private
boundary, unsupported version, invalid provenance/custody, broken freeze/lineage,
scope drift, missing negative evidence, semantic conflict, nondeterminism,
self-review, unsafe rollback or absent PO authority. No score, deadline, report
or technical success grants closure or implementation authority.
