1. M9.1 Product Shell & Navigation.
2. M9.2 Player Profile & Progress.
3. M9.3 Training Session Workspace.
4. M9.4 Coach Context & Decision View.
5. M9.5 Plan & Recommendation Inbox.

Each capability requires a focused contract/adapter test plan, full regression,
Architecture Fitness, protected M3-M8 validation, Product Owner review, and
commit/push only after acceptance. No M9 capability may introduce a platform
contract without an explicit new ADR and Product Owner approval.
