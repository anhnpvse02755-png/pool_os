# M22 Platform Closure Plan

**Status:** Accepted Planning Baseline; Closed
**Date:** 2026-07-23

## Closure Contract

M22 performs final validation of the frozen platform and defines the governed
prerequisites for a future Product transition. It authorizes no Product feature,
runtime implementation, release or deployment.

## Dependency-Ordered Milestones

### M22.1 Final Closure Identity & Scope
Bind the exact M21 root, final claims, exclusions, authorities and lineage.

### M22.2 Constitutional & Ownership Validation
Confirm constitutional invariants, dependency direction and semantic owners.

### M22.3 Freeze Chain & Public Contract Validation
Validate M3-M21 continuity and every Product-facing public boundary/version.

### M22.4 Evidence, Provenance & Replay Closure
Require complete source-owned positive/negative evidence and deterministic replay.

### M22.5 Operational, Security & Recovery Closure
Validate trust, privacy, operations, capacity, recovery and failure semantics.

### M22.6 Product Transition Prerequisites & Ownership
Define only allowed public consumption, ownership and future authorization gates.

### M22.7 Independent Final Validation & Gap Closure
Independently validate all claims and close or block every residual item.

### M22.8 Final Platform Closure Gate
Record binary eligibility for separately authorized M22 Foundation Freeze only.

## Invariants

M21 Freeze is the direct root and M3-M21 remain protected. Completion is
conjunctive, source-owned, deterministic, independently validated and fail-
closed. Product remains locked until M22 Foundation Freeze is accepted, closed,
committed and pushed. Every M22.x requires separate PO authority.
