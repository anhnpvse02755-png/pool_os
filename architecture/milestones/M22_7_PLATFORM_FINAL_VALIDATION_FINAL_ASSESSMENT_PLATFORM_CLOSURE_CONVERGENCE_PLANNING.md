## Immutable Final Platform Assessment Model

An assessment binds assessment ID/type/schema, candidate/scope/M21 root,
accepted M22 plan identities and lineage, criteria/evidence/dependency/gap/
audit/risk/transition digests, completion and convergence rules, ordered
determinations, unresolved items/limitations/exceptions, owners/verifiers/
authorities, validity, rollback/repair/supersession, provenance and digest.
Any bound change creates a successor; reports/scores cannot inherit readiness.

## Immutable Closure Convergence Model

Convergence binds exact assessment, closure identity/scope, all source package
digests, bidirectional semantic relationships, conjunctive dimension states,
evidence coverage, unresolved dependencies, ownership/approval chain,
independent verification, validity and digest. It records planning state only and
cannot close the platform, create M22 Freeze or authorize Product.

## Deterministic M22.1-M22.6 Convergence

Convergence requires exact alignment among M22.1 identity/scope, M22.2 evidence/
criteria, M22.3 graph/gaps, M22.4 governance/audit, M22.5 risks/exceptions and
M22.6 transition/handoff. Every reference resolves bidirectionally to the same
candidate, scope and M21 root. Missing, mixed, stale, duplicate, orphaned or
contradictory bindings block.

Records order by dimension, criterion, claim, source and record ID. A mandatory
stable topological order governs dependencies. Same inputs produce the same
assessment JSON, convergence state and digest independent of input order.

1. candidate, closure identity and exact scope;
2. M21 root and M3-M21 freeze-lineage continuity;

4. positive/negative evidence, provenance and custody;
5. deterministic dependencies, gap and finding closure;

## Unresolved Dependency Governance

Every unresolved item binds ID/class, predecessor/successor, affected criteria,
source state, owner, evidence, impact, blocking dependents, escalation,
permitted resolution, validity and digest. Unknown ownership, cycle, private/
reversed dependency, incompatible version, open gap/finding/risk, invalid
exception, incomplete handoff or conflicting truth remains blocking.

## Source-Owned Evidence Convergence

Every claim requires current positive/negative evidence, semantic ID/digest,
source/custodian, method/rule/contract versions, canonical results, validity,
limitations, correction lineage and independent verification. Coverage is by
exact claim, never aggregate test count. Indexes/reports reference evidence at
source and cannot self-review, manufacture evidence or become authority.

## Deterministic Assessment Sequence

Stages are `identity`, `lineage`, `scope`, `evidence`, `dependencies`,
`semantics`, `governanceAudit`, `riskTrustOperations`, `transitionHandoff`,

## Rollback Repair Supersession And Fail-Closed Governance

Rollback selects a verified predecessor and revokes dependent eligibility
without editing history. Repair occurs only at source under separate authority,
followed by downstream revalidation. Supersession links all sources,
assessments, findings and decisions with reason/impact/digests.

Reject wrong/mixed/stale identity, broken lineage, incomplete scope/evidence,
cycle, unresolved dependency, conflict, open gap/finding/risk, invalid exception,
duty conflict, invalid custody, nondeterminism, self-review, unsafe rollback,
incomplete handoff or broken freeze. Eligibility is only for separately
authorized M22.8 planning and grants no validation, closure, freeze or Product.
