# M21.8 Platform Final Readiness Gate Planning

**Status:** Accepted; Closed
**Date:** 2026-07-23

## Objective

Define the immutable final readiness gate for accepted M21 planning. Planning
only; no Product feature, runtime contract, implementation, UI, deployment,
CI/CD, monitoring, infrastructure, tooling, ADR, freeze or generated protected-
artifact change is authorized.

## Authority And Protected Inputs

Constitution v1.4.0, accepted M21.0-M21.7 and M20 Foundation Freeze remain
protected. Source milestones retain semantic ownership. M21.8 binds exact IDs
and digests, verifies the final conjunction and cannot reinterpret, repair,
widen, execute or freeze their claims.

## Immutable Final Readiness Gate Identity

Gate identity binds gate ID/type/schema, exact candidate/completion scope, M20
root, accepted M21.0-M21.7 identities, criteria/rule-set versions, evidence index,
dependency/gap, audit/finding, risk/exception, transition/handoff and final
assessment digests, authorities, validity, predecessor/successor and gate digest.

Any bound change creates a successor and invalidates prior verification. Names,
paths, timestamps, reports, aggregate test counts, provider output and repository
status cannot substitute for semantic identity or inherit eligibility.

## Immutable Final Readiness Gate Model

A gate package contains exact identity, normalized input manifest, ordered
criterion determinations, evidence-reference index, independent verification,
limitations/open-item index, authorization record, recovery boundary and
append-only lineage. It exposes only `eligibleForM21Freeze` or `blocked`.

The state is derived, not manually assigned. `eligibleForM21Freeze` means only
that a separately authorized M21 Foundation Freeze may be planned; it creates no
freeze, M22, Product, runtime, release, deployment or implementation authority.

## Conjunctive Gate Criteria

1. M20 protected root and all accepted M21 milestone identities are exact.
2. Candidate/completion scope and inclusion/exclusion are complete.
3. Ten M21.2 criteria have current positive and negative evidence.
4. Dependency graph is complete, deterministic and acyclic.
5. All required gaps and audit findings are independently closed.
6. Governance, separation of duties and authority chains are valid.
7. No non-acceptable/open residual risk or invalid exception remains.
8. Operational transition, evidence transfer and handoff are acknowledged.
9. M21.7 convergence is current, complete and independently verified.
10. Protected freezes, replay, rollback and audit continuity remain intact.

All criteria are mandatory and non-compensating. Not-applicable requires exact
source proof and independent verification. No score, deadline, majority, report,
exception or technical success can offset a failed criterion.

## Evidence Aggregation By Immutable Reference

The gate index stores semantic evidence ID/digest, exact claim/criterion, source
and custodian, contract/rule versions, positive/negative state, validity,
correction/supersession lineage, independent review and limitations. Evidence
remains source-owned; no content is copied or regenerated as authority.

References order by criterion, claim, source and evidence ID. Duplicate semantic
references, mixed candidates, stale digests, missing negative evidence, invalid
custody or orphan lineage block the gate. Same inputs yield the same index and
digest independent of supplied collection order.

## Independent Gate Verification

Verification binds exact gate/input/rule digests, verifier identity and
independence proof, ordered checks, findings, limitations, validity and digest.
The verifier cannot assemble the candidate, own/remediate audited claims,
produce evidence, request/approve exceptions or issue PO authorization.

The deterministic sequence is `identity`, `scope`, `lineage`, `evidence`,
`dependencies`, `closure`, `governanceAudit`, `riskTransition`, `convergence`
and `finalConjunction`. A failed prerequisite blocks all downstream eligibility.

## Ownership And Authorization Boundaries

| Responsibility | Accountable authority |
|---|---|
| Gate composition and canonical ordering | Architecture/Platform |
| Source truth and corrections | Owning domain |
| Contract/version compatibility | Producer and consumer owners |
| Evidence/provenance/custody | Source/Evidence owner |
| Dependency/gap closure | Affected owners and independent verifier |
| Audit/finding conclusion | Independent Quality/auditor |
| Risk/exception acceptance | Explicit risk/governance authority |
| Transition/handoff acknowledgement | Source and receiving owners |
| Final gate verification | Independent gate verifier |
| M21 Freeze planning eligibility | Product Owner |

Authorization binds exact gate digest, scope, authority mandate and validity.
Repository operations, automation, generated output or a passing test cannot
approve the gate. PO eligibility cannot bypass a failed criterion.

## Rollback, Repair And Supersession

Rollback selects a verified compatible predecessor gate package and revokes
dependent eligibility without editing history. Repair occurs only at the source
under separate authority, then all affected downstream criteria are reverified.
Supersession links old/new gate, source, evidence, finding and decision digests
with reason and impact. Failed and rejected gates remain auditable.

## Fail-Closed Governance

Reject mixed/stale/duplicate identity, incomplete lineage/scope/evidence,
unsupported version, cycle, open gap/finding/risk, invalid exception, duty
conflict, self-review, invalid custody, semantic conflict, nondeterminism,
incomplete handoff, unsafe rollback, broken freeze or scope drift. There is no
fallback, provisional pass or partial eligibility.

Eligibility requires all ten criteria, current independent verification and a
separate PO decision. It is limited to M21 Foundation Freeze planning only and
does not itself authorize creating or modifying freeze artifacts.
