## Compatibility

- Existing typed `requires` remains the backward-compatible LR-2 form.
- An entry cannot mix typed `requires` and `unlock` authoring.
- Existing entries without `unlock` keep their generated bytes and behavior.
- Production Knowledge `0.2.1`, current, LR-2, M2.3, and M2.4 identities are
  unchanged.
- Publication schema/workflow, Evidence, Golden Fixtures, Reference Behavior,
  and Constitution are unchanged.

## Trace Semantics

Dependency leaves retain `PREREQUISITE_SATISFIED` or
`PREREQUISITE_UNSATISFIED` and add `expressionNodeId`. Every AND node emits
`UNLOCK_EXPRESSION_SATISFIED` or `UNLOCK_EXPRESSION_UNSATISFIED` with its node
ID and failed child node IDs. No explanation prose is embedded.

## Scope Boundary

LR-4 implements only `allOf`/AND. OR, NOT, XOR, arbitrary boolean operators,
probabilistic mastery, dynamic categories, attempt-level Evidence, corpus
migration, and production activation remain out of scope. Engineering closure
does not imply Product acceptance.
