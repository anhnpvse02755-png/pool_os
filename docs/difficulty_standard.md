## 1. Overview

This document defines the difficulty classification system for the BKM Knowledge Base. Each difficulty level represents a distinct stage of skill development, with clear evaluation criteria that ensure consistent categorization across all content.

## 4. Level Definitions

### 4.1 Beginner

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                              BEGINNER                                        │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    Definition:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    Players who are new to billiards or have limited experience. They are 
    learning fundamental mechanics and basic shot types.

    Target Audience:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    • First-time players
    • Casual players with < 6 months experience
    • Players focused on recreation
    • Individuals learning fundamentals

    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    EVALUATION CRITERIA

    Technical Skill:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    □ Can execute basic stance and grip
    □ Can perform open bridge consistently
    □ Can make straight-in shots to pockets
    □ Understands fundamental shot types (draw, follow, stop)
    □ Basic cue ball control on simple shots

    Tactical Knowledge:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    □ Understands basic rules of 8-ball
    □ Can identify open balls vs solids
    □ Knows what a "easy" shot looks like
    □ Understands fouls and basic penalties
    □ Can identify the cue ball

    Situational Awareness:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    □ Can identify a ball near a pocket
    □ Understands what "pocket" means
    □ Can see the line of the shot
    □ Recognizes obvious obstacles
    □ Knows when a ball is in the pocket

    Mental Game:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    □ Can focus on one shot at a time
    □ Can handle simple pressure (friends watching)
    □ Shows willingness to learn
    □ Accepts mistakes as part of learning
    □ Enjoys the game despite losses

    Breadth of Knowledge:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    □ Knows 5-10 basic shot types
    □ Understands basic equipment (cue, balls, table)
    □ Knows table etiquette
    □ Understands basic scoring
    □ Familiar with terminology (spot, pocket, shot)

    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    Performance Benchmarks:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    • Can pocket 3-5 balls per game (8-ball)
    • Makes straight shots (1-2 feet) at 40-60% rate
    • Occasionally executes draw/follow intentionally
    • Rarely scratches on intentional safeties
    • Understands game flow

    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    Content Complexity:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    • Simple vocabulary, no jargon
    • Short, clear sentences
    • Concrete, relatable examples
    • Step-by-step instructions
    • Encouraging tone
    • Visual aids highly recommended

    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    Example Skill Indicators:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    ✓ "I can make a straight shot most of the time"
    ✓ "I know what the cue ball does"
    ✓ "I understand 8-ball rules"
    ✗ "I can execute advanced position play"
    ✗ "I understand spin transfer mechanics"
```

### 4.2 Intermediate

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                            INTERMEDIATE                                       │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    Definition:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    Players with established fundamentals who are developing consistency, 
    expanding technique range, and beginning strategic play.

    Target Audience:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    • Players with 6 months to 2 years regular play
    • Players who compete occasionally
    • Players seeking to improve consistency
    • Those who understand basics and want more

    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    EVALUATION CRITERIA

    Technical Skill:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    □ Can execute draw, follow, and stop shots consistently
    □ Uses closed bridge when appropriate
    □ Can control cue ball position on basic shots
    □ Understands english (side spin) basics
    □ Can execute medium-distance shots (3-5 feet)
    □ Basic speed control (soft, medium, firm)

    Tactical Knowledge:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    □ Can identify " percentage shots" (30%, 50%, etc.)
    □ Understands position play basics
    □ Knows when to play safe vs. shoot
    □ Can plan 1-2 shots ahead
    □ Understands break strategy
    □ Knows basic safety play principles

    Situational Awareness:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    □ Can read simple cloth conditions
    □ Recognizes common foul situations
    □ Identifies clusters and problem areas
    □ Understands kick shots and bank shot basics
    □ Can assess table layout strategically

    Mental Game:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    □ Maintains focus through a rack
    □ Can recover from mistakes within a game
    □ Understands match flow (momentum)
    □ Can handle moderate competitive pressure
    □ Shows patience in difficult situations

    Breadth of Knowledge:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    □ Knows 15-25 techniques
    □ Understands equipment differences (tips, shafts)
    □ Familiar with tournament rules variations
    □ Knows history and major players
    □ Understands practice methodologies

    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    Performance Benchmarks:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    • Makes straight shots (3-5 feet) at 60-75% rate
    • Executes draw/follow consistently at 50-60%
    • Pockets 5-8 balls per game regularly
    • Can run 2-3 balls in sequence
    • Win rate 40-60% against similar players

    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    Content Complexity:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    • Moderate vocabulary with explanations
    • Technical terms introduced with context
    • Comparative explanations
    • Strategic depth added
    • Multiple examples per concept
    • Some nuance in instructions

    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    Example Skill Indicators:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    ✓ "I can control where the cue ball goes after a shot"
    ✓ "I know when to play safe instead of shooting"
    ✓ "I understand position play"
    ✗ "I can execute advanced english combinations"
    ✗ "I consistently win local tournaments"
```

## 7. Content Difficulty Assignment

### 7.1 Assignment Guidelines

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        CONTENT ASSIGNMENT GUIDELINES                          │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    Assign difficulty based on the answer to:
    
    "What level of player needs this content to improve?"

    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    Assign BEGINNER when:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    • Content is about fundamentals
    • Skills are prerequisite to everything else
    • Language must be accessible to first-timers
    • No prior knowledge assumed
    • Basic equipment or setup instructions

    Assign INTERMEDIATE when:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    • Building on established fundamentals
    • Adding consistency and variation
    • Introducing strategy alongside technique
    • Player has options and needs decision-making
    • Content applies to regular recreational improvement

    Assign ADVANCED when:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    • Technique refinement for skilled players
    • Complex multi-variable skills
    • High-level strategic concepts
    • Requires solid foundation to understand
    • Tournament preparation content

    Assign PROFESSIONAL when:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    • Elite technique mastery
    • Competition psychology
    • Tournament-specific strategies
    • Equipment optimization at high level
    • Content for players with proven competitive results

    Assign EXPERT when:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    • Historical or legendary technique analysis
    • Innovation and theory development
    • Teaching methodology for elite coaches
    • Philosophical or game theory content
    • Content representing pinnacle achievement
```

## 8. Difficulty Transition Guide

### 8.1 Beginner to Intermediate

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    TRANSITION: BEGINNER → INTERMEDIATE                        │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    Prerequisites:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    • Consistent straight shot execution (60%+)
    • Understanding of all basic shots
    • Basic 8-ball strategy awareness
    • Regular practice routine established

    Key Skills to Develop:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    • Draw/follow/stop consistency
    • Basic english application
    • Shot planning (1-2 ahead)
    • Basic safety recognition
    • Distance control

    Assessment:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    ✓ Can execute draw shot on demand at 50%+
    ✓ Makes 3-foot percentage shots consistently
    ✓ Recognizes when to play safe
    ✓ Won against other beginners regularly
    ✓ Practices 2-3 times per week
```

### 8.2 Intermediate to Advanced

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                  TRANSITION: INTERMEDIATE → ADVANCED                          │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    Prerequisites:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    • 60-75% on standard shots
    • Consistent draw/follow at medium distances
    • Basic kick and bank execution
    • Tournament experience
    • Structured practice routine

    Key Skills to Develop:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    • Advanced english mastery
    • Complex position play
    • Multi-ball planning
    • Advanced safety play
    • Kick and bank proficiency

    Assessment:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    ✓ 75%+ on percentage shots
    ✓ Runs 5-7 balls regularly
    ✓ Complex safety exchanges won
    ✓ Top 3 finishes in local tournaments
    ✓ Consistent kick/bank execution
```

### 8.3 Advanced to Professional

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   TRANSITION: ADVANCED → PROFESSIONAL                         │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    Prerequisites:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    • 85%+ on standard shots
    • Advanced technique mastery
    • Regional tournament success
    • Elite mental game
    • Full technique repertoire

    Key Skills to Develop:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    • Push the limits of execution
    • Tournament-specific tactics
    • Opponent exploitation
    • Advanced pattern play
    • Competition psychology mastery

    Assessment:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    ✓ Regional tournament wins
    ✓ 90%+ on high-pressure shots
    ✓ Consistent major final appearances
    ✓ Advanced training methodology
    ✓ Teaching or coaching ability
```

### 8.4 Professional to Expert

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   TRANSITION: PROFESSIONAL → EXPERT                          │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    Prerequisites:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    • Multiple major tournament wins
    • World ranking
    • Revolutionary skill or strategy
    • Teaching/coaching mastery
    • Game theory contribution

    Key Skills to Develop:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    • Perfect consistency
    • Innovation and legacy
    • Teaching methodology
    • Historical game evolution
    • Mentorship and influence

    Assessment:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    ✓ World Championship titles
    ✓ Historical significance
    ✓ Revolutionized aspects of the game
    ✓ Hall of Fame consideration
    ✓ Generational influence
```

## 9. Self-Assessment Guide

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                          SELF-ASSESSMENT GUIDE                                │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    Answer these questions honestly:

    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    SHOT EXECUTION
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    □ What percentage of straight-in shots do I make?
    □ Can I execute draw, follow, and stop on command?
    □ How far can I control the cue ball accurately?
    □ Do I adjust for table conditions?

    STRATEGY
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    □ How many shots ahead can I plan?
    □ When do I play safe vs. shoot?
    □ Can I win safety battles consistently?
    □ Do I have a tournament strategy?

    TOURNAMENT PERFORMANCE
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    □ What are my tournament results?
    □ How do I perform under pressure?
    □ Can I recover from mistakes mid-match?
    □ What is my win rate against different levels?

    KNOWLEDGE
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    □ How many techniques can I execute?
    □ Do I understand equipment differences?
    □ Can I teach these concepts to others?
    □ Do I understand the mental game?

    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    Scoring:
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    Beginner:    Mostly "I can't do this yet"
    Intermediate: Mixed, growing capability
    Advanced:    Strong capability, some gaps
    Professional: Strong across all areas
    Expert:      Perfect or near-perfect across all
```
