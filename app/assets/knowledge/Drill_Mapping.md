## Strategy Training Progressions

### Basic Strategy Progression

1. **Foundation** (1-2 weeks)
   - Learn basic shots (STROKE001, AIM001, GB001)
   - Understand rules and game flow

2. **Fundamentals** (2-4 weeks)
   - Master stroke basics (PEND001, SPEED001)
   - Learn position concept (P001)
   - Basic safety play (S001)

3. **Intermediate** (1-2 months)
   - Bank and kick shots (ORB001, ORK001)
   - Cue ball control (STOP001, DRAW001)
   - Run out basics (RUN001)

4. **Advanced** (2-4 months)
   - Complex shots (EDGE001, MASSE001)
   - Strategic play (SS001, PCT001)
   - Match pressure (MATCH002)

5. **Professional** (4+ months)
   - All skills combined (MASTER001, MASTER002)
   - Expert level (PRESS001)
   - Continuous improvement (ITER002)
